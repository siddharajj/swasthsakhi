# 🌸 SwasthSakhi — Women's Preventive Health, Screening Plan & Awareness Hub (India)

A responsive, privacy-first Progressive Web App (PWA) built to close the preventive health and cancer screening gap for women in India — spanning **adolescence (13–19)** through **reproductive years, pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause, menopause, post-menopause, and senior years (65+)**.

🌐 **Live Web App:** https://siddharajj.github.io/swasthsakhi/

---

## Why SwasthSakhi?
- **98.1%** of Indian women aged 30–49 have *never* had cervical cancer screening, and **99.1%** have never had a clinical breast exam (*NFHS-5, MoHFW India*).
- **57.0%** of women (15–49) and **59.1%** of teenage girls in India live with anemia.
- Indian women reach natural menopause **4–5 years earlier** (~46.2 years) than Western clinical defaults (51–52 years), requiring earlier bone density (DEXA), cardiometabolic, and endometrial awareness.

## User Journey & Features

1. **Awareness & Data Story Hook**
   - Highlights national screening gaps (*NFHS-5, ICMR, FOGSI & Indian Menopause Society*), regional disparities, the **Post-Menopausal Bleeding & Endometrial Thickening** spotlight, *Cervavac* indigenous HPV vaccine & *HPV Self-Sampling* breakthroughs, and **1-tap WhatsApp Share Cards**.
   - Visitors can explore a **Live Interactive Demo** or build their own personalised plan in 60 seconds.

2. **Guided 3-Step Profile Builder & Bento Health Care Dashboard**
   - Covers **9 life stages** (*Teenage, Pre-Menopausal, Pre-Pregnancy, Pregnancy, Postpartum, Perimenopause, Menopause, Post-Menopause, Senior 65+*) and **10 condition overlays** (*PCOS, Thyroid, Anemia, Fibroids/Endometriosis, Past Post-Menopausal Bleeding/Endometrial Thickening, Diabetes/GDM, Hypertension, Osteopenia/Osteoporosis, Familial Cancer Risk, Vegetarian B12 focus*).
   - **India Lab & Legal Access Badges**:
     - `🏠 Home / Walk-In Lab` — Direct NABL pathology lab or home phlebotomy without needing a GP prescription (*Lal PathLabs, Metropolis, Thyrocare, Tata 1mg, Apollo, Redcliffe*).
     - `🏥 Clinic / Imaging Center` — Mammogram/Sono-mammography, DEXA scan, Pap/HPV DNA test, Pelvic/TVS Ultrasound.
     - `📋 Doctor Rx Mandatory` — Specialist care & legally regulated scans (including **PC-PNDT Act Form F + ID** requirement for all pregnancy ultrasounds).
   - **Smart Batching & Yearly Schedule** — Groups annual checks into *1 Morning Fasting Home Draw* + *1 Half-Day Clinic/Imaging Visit* with realistic ₹ INR combo estimates and an expandable **Symptom Safety Net**.

3. **Non-Medical Test Result Literacy Analyser**
   - Strictly educational, non-diagnostic interpretation across **11 blood & imaging markers** (*Hemoglobin, Serum Ferritin, TSH, HbA1c, Fasting Glucose, Fasting Insulin + HOMA-IR, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, LDL Cholesterol, TVS Endometrial Thickness in mm, and DEXA T-score*).
   - Includes a **Quick Paste Parser** for standard Indian pathology report text and generates a printable/shareable **Doctor Visit Prep Card**.

4. **Inclusive Access, Privacy & All 22 Indian Languages**
   - **100% Client-Side Privacy**: Profiles and test values remain locally in the user's browser (`localStorage`) with optional `.json` backup/restore.
   - **Login-Free or 3-Word Sakhi Key**: Works without accounts/passwords, with an optional memorable 3-word key (`tulsi-pearl-sunrise`), Family Circle multi-profile switching, `A+` elder-friendly text scaling, and support across **all 22 scheduled Indian languages**.
